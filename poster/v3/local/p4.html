Check cost, and what elision wins back

Outline: slowdown from all bounds checks (checks on vs. off).  Fill: the part elision removes.
Where checks are expensive and provable, elision wins back all of their cost, on both GPUs. On NVIDIA, vello’s checks are nearly free, so there is little to win. Blur’s checks cost 7% on Intel but are mostly not provable: the coverage limit in panel 5.
Exploratory (raw medians, not pre-registered). Synthetic: every kernel with a measurable check cost recovers 95–100% (5 on NVIDIA, 3 on Intel). Why checks cost more on Intel is not yet known.
