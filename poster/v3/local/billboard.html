Proving array indices in bounds removes 102 of the 186 bounds clamps in vello’s GPU shaders.
On an Intel iGPU that wins back the full cost of the checks: +7.0%. On NVIDIA the checks cost only 1.3% in total, so the gain is small: +0.6%.
+7.0% / +0.6%: pre-registered suite geomeans over 32 vello scenes each (95% CI +3.7 to +10.4% and +0.2 to +1.0%). Total check cost (all checks vs. none) is exploratory.
