Where checks are expensive and provable, elision wins back all of their cost, on both GPUs. On NVIDIA, vello’s checks are nearly free. Blur’s checks cost 7% on Intel but are mostly not provable (section 5).
Exploratory: raw medians, not pre-registered. Every synthetic kernel with a measurable check cost recovers 95–100% (5 on NVIDIA, 3 on Intel). Why checks cost more on Intel is not yet known.
